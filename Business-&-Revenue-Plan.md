## 1. Executive Summary

GreenMind is a technology-enabled vertical farming service company providing customers with:

1. Vertical-farming equipment
2. Installation and system configuration
3. Automated irrigation and environmental-control equipment
4. LED grow-light control
5. IoT sensors and controllers
6. Real-time web dashboard
7. AI-powered plant recommendations
8. Plant-growth analytics
9. Manual fertilizer/nutrient recommendations
10. Scheduled physical maintenance
11. Technical support
12. Optional future upgrades and replacement components

The customer makes a one-time payment for the equipment and installation and then pays a recurring subscription for GreenMind's monitoring and intelligence platform.

The recommended business model is:

> **Hardware + Installation + SaaS Subscription + Annual Maintenance + Optional Services**

This provides GreenMind with both immediate revenue and recurring revenue.

GreenMind does not simply sell a vertical farm.

GreenMind provides a complete system that helps customers operate a vertical farm with less manual monitoring.

# 24. Should GreenMind Patent the Idea?

### Not yet — at least not simply "the GreenMind idea."

You cannot effectively patent:

> "An IoT system that monitors plants and gives AI recommendations."

That concept is too broad and there is already extensive prior art.

MyIPO states that a patentable invention must be new, involve an inventive step and be industrially applicable. Business methods, mathematical methods and other excluded subject matter are not patentable.

Malaysia already has publicly documented smart-agriculture technologies involving IoT, AI, hydroponics, sensors, dashboards and automation, so you should expect substantial prior art in this field.

# 26. Patent Timing Is Important

Do not publicly disclose a potentially patentable invention before discussing the filing strategy with a patent professional.

MyIPO says a patent invention must be new and must not have been publicly disclosed.

So before:

* publishing technical documentation
* posting detailed schematics
* presenting the invention publicly
* releasing technical papers
* showing investors detailed proprietary mechanisms

you should determine whether there is anything worth protecting.

MyIPO's current official fees show a basic patent application fee of RM290 and substantive examination of RM1,100, although professional patent-agent costs and other expenses are additional.

MyIPO also states that patent applications are confidential until publication, normally 18 months from the relevant priority/filing date.

# 32. Final Recommendation

GreenMind's business model is fundamentally sound.

I would keep:

**One-time equipment + installation**

**Monthly dashboard/AI subscription**

**Annual physical maintenance**

But add:

**Hardware warranty**

**Replacement parts**

**Premium analytics**

**Optional expansion modules**

and eventually:

**Consumables/nutrient packages**

if you decide to handle them.

Most importantly, don't try to compete with established Malaysian companies by building "another vertical farm."

Build:

> **GreenMind — the intelligent operating system and automation layer for vertical farms.**

The physical farm demonstrates the technology.

The software creates recurring revenue.

The AI creates differentiation.

The maintenance creates customer retention.

The hardware creates the initial sale.

That is a much stronger company model.

### My recommendation for the immediate next step

For the prototype, I would **not start by buying all the equipment**. We should first design **GreenMind Prototype V1** down to the exact component level: **which plant, number of plants, rack dimensions, hydroponic method, every sensor, ESP32/other controller, pump, LED, relay/MOSFET, power supply, wiring, estimated Malaysian price, and exact data flow into your existing GreenMind website.**

That will give you a realistic **RM5k–RM10k prototype budget** and a build plan you can actually follow.
